# Series bible: Night shift radio
- World: a 24 hour gas station shop (kiosk) at night. HUD shows DAY 41, STORE 24H, RULES: 2.
- Rules: 1) Never open the door after midnight. 2) Never answer the radio.
- Hero: "you", the grey tube-limb stick figure, the night-shift worker.
- Radio: small green handheld radio on the counter. It glows green when the voice speaks.
- Sister: died on day 9, "you watched it happen". Her voice on the radio says "Please let me in. I'm right outside." and says the hero's real name. (Use a female voice for her in later parts.)
- Threat: green zombie-like figures in the fog outside, and something smiling behind the glass door.
- Part 1 ended on a vote: ANSWER the radio or stay SILENT. SILENT won.
- Part 2 (made 27 Oct): SILENT chosen. A mimic behind the glass door of the kiosk, sister's voice, ends on a RUN or STAY vote. (Part 2 was made without this file; check its folder when it exists.)
